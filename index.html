The Premise

You don't climb the Tower.
You administer it.

Once every cycle, the Tower reactivates. Something inside it calls out — and things answer. They call themselves Resonants. Nobody remembers who named them that first.

Nine rooms to a floor. Seven fights, one merchant, one door that isn't like the others. What's waiting up there changes depending on how deep you've gone, and how deep is a question the Tower answers differently for everyone.

We're not going to explain the rest. Better if you find it.

The Wanderer

"I don't care what the Tower wants. I'm finding her."

Pulled through a door that shouldn't have opened, chasing a close friend who went through it first. He's climbing because she's still up there — not for glory, and not for anything the Tower thinks he should want.

The Signature Mechanic

Play it once, it's just a card.
Play it three times, it's a Combinite.

Every card in your deck is hiding something. Line the same one up three times in a row and it stops being a card — it becomes a named attack, with its own cutscene, its own line of dialogue, its own reason to exist. There are dozens of these. We're only going to show you how it feels, not what they are.
